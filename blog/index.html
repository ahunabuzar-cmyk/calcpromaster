What you'll find here

The guides library teaches a specific calculation top to bottom; the blog takes a single idea the calculator surfaced and follows it to the conclusion. Why stacked discounts never add up the way the sign implies. Why an EMI that never changes actually pays two different things at once. Why one screening number describes millions of bodies and still shouldn't be read as a personal verdict. Each post is short on purpose — one idea, the formula behind it, and the numbers that prove it — and every claim can be checked with the linked calculator.

Posts are organized by what they help you decide, not by tool category: money posts deal with borrowing, discounting and salary math; health posts deal with the metrics people quote at each other.
