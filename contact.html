What to Include in Your Message

To help us respond quickly, please include:

  • For bug reports: Which calculator, what you entered, what result you expected vs. what you got, and your browser/device
  • For calculator requests: What you want to calculate and why existing calculators don't meet your needs
  • For partnerships: Your brand/company, the type of collaboration, and a sample link or placement you have in mind
  • For general feedback: Be specific! "The mortgage calculator is confusing" is less helpful than "The mortgage calculator should show monthly breakdown"
