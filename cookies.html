Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. CalcProMaster uses minimal cookies and primarily relies on localStorage (similar to cookies but stored only in your browser).

What We Use

Essential (Always Active):

  • localStorage: Stores your calculation history, favorites, preferences, and settings. This is NOT a cookie and never leaves your device.
  • Cookie consent choice: Remembers whether you accepted or rejected non-essential cookies.

Non-Essential (Your Choice):

  • Google Analytics cookies: If you consent, Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage statistics (which calculators are popular, page views, etc.). You can opt out via our cookie banner.
  • Google AdSense cookies: If you consent, Google AdSense uses cookies for ad personalization. You can opt out via our cookie banner or use an ad blocker.

We Do NOT Use:

  • Tracking cookies that follow you across websites
  • Third-party marketing cookies (except Google Analytics/AdSense if you consent)
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Fingerprinting or other invasive tracking methods

Your Choices

You can control cookies via:

  • Our cookie banner: Appears on your first visit. Choose "Accept All" or "Reject Non-Essential".
  • Browser settings: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Note: Blocking essential localStorage will prevent favorites/history from working.
  • Ad blockers: Browser extensions can block advertising and tracking cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

If you consent, Google Analytics and Google AdSense may set their own cookies. Their use is governed by:

  • Google Cookie Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
