Health & Medical Disclaimer

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

Important Notice

CalcProMaster health calculators provide general estimates for informational purposes only. They are NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Not Medical Advice

The results generated by CalcProMaster health calculators should never be used to self-diagnose or make medical decisions. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider before:

  • Starting any diet, exercise, or weight loss program
  • Making changes to your medication or treatment
  • Diagnosing or treating any medical condition
  • Making decisions about your health or fitness

General Estimates Only

Health calculators use standardized formulas that may not be accurate for:

  • Individuals with medical conditions
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding women
  • Children and adolescents
  • Athletes or highly active individuals
  • Elderly individuals
  • People with unique body compositions

No Substitute for Professional Care

CalcProMaster calculators cannot:

  • Diagnose medical conditions
  • Recommend specific treatments or medications
  • Replace blood tests, medical imaging, or clinical assessments
  • Account for your individual medical history

Emergency Situations

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately. Do not rely on any online calculator.

How Our Health Calculations Work (Methodology)

Our health tools use formulas published in peer-reviewed research and clinical practice — for example, Mifflin-St Jeor for basal metabolic rate, the standard BMI weight/height ratio, and established heart-rate zone percentages. Each calculator displays its formula, a worked example, and its assumptions on the page, and states its limits: the population ranges a formula was derived from, what activity multipliers assume, and when a result should be discussed with a clinician. All calculations run entirely in your browser — no health data is sent anywhere.

  • Following professional medical advice
