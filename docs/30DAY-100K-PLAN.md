## 1. Math — 100k kahan se aayega

```
impressions ≈ indexed pages × avg impr/page/week × 4 hafte
100,000     ≈ 1,300+ indexed pages × 15–25 impr/page/week × 4 hafte
              + regional cluster (proven: dubai-salary 217 impr/28d akela)
              + directories/PH/Reddit referral jo brand-queries banate hain
```

- Har tool page pe EASY phrase + FAQ/HowTo schema + deep content already hai (QA-tested).
- **Sab se bara blocker indexing velocity hai** — iske 4 levers: (1) IndexNow repings,
  (2) internal links, (3) backlinks (directories live ho rahe), (4) crawl-fresh pages
  (naye regional pages har hafte deploy).
- **Regional cluster asli engine hai:** dubai-salary pattern ko 5+5+5... pages se scale karna.
  Har batch = new crawl trigger + long-tail impressions.
- Honest odds (sab kuch execute hua to):
  - 25,000+ impr: ~95%
  - 50,000+ impr: ~70%
  - **100,000: ~45–55%** — weeks 3–4 pe directories + PH + YouTube boosters decide karenge.
    Pivot rule (§6) isliye har checkpoint pe hai.
