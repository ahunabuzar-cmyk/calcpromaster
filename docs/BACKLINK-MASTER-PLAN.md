# CalcProMaster - Backlink Master Plan (Quality-First, Google-Safe)

Generated 2026-10-04 from every existing list in docs/ (500-pack + 1000-queue), de-duplicated by domain and split into honest quality tiers.

Total unique free targets found: **505**  (Tier A 27 / Tier B 170 / Tier C 207 / do-not-use 101).

## 0. Pehle sach: "2000 backlinks" kyun galat target hai

Main seedha batauunga, kyunki aap ne khud kaha tha "Google ka rule break na ho":

- **2000 hand-verified high-quality free sites exist nahi karte.** Duniya me genuinely acchi, free, relevant backlink sources ki tadaad sainkdon me hai, hazaron me nahi.
- **2000 mass submissions = link scheme.** Google ki spam policy (link spam) me "excessive link exchanges" aur "low-quality directory/profile links at scale" saaf likha hai. Ye aap ke naye domain par **manual action / devalue penalty** ka seedha risk hai.
- **Naye domain par spike = red flag.** 2-hafte purane site par ek hafte me 2000 links = algorithm ke liye unnatural. Isi liye velocity cap zaroori hai.
Toh is doc ka number 2000 nahi - ye **505 real targets** hain, aur in me se bhi sirf **Tier A + B (~197)** worth doing hain. Ye zyada honest hai aur zyada mehfooz.

## 1. Tee link engines (asli scaling yahin se aati hai)

### Engine 1 - Directories & communities (ye doc)

One-off listings. Har ek = ek backlink. Safe, par limited. Ye base trust layer banati hain, shortcut nahi.

### Engine 2 - Link magnets (ye hazaron links de sakta hai, white-hat)

- Embeddable calculator widgets (aap ka /embed/ already mojood) - har blogger jahan embed kare = ek natural editorial link. Ye "2000" ka **legit** raasta hai.
- Free data study / infographic (e.g. "cost of living calculator data 2026") - journalists aur bloggers khud link karte hain.
- Open-source repo + awesome-list PRs - developer community link karti hai.
### Engine 3 - Editorial / digital PR

HARO-style quotes, guest posts, resource-page outreach. Nofollow ya dofollow - dono brand + crawl signal dete hain.

## 2. Safe daily cadence (aap 4-5/din chahte the)

4-5/din chalega, **par sirf mixed tiers me**, aur pehle 2 hafte dheere:

- Week 1-2: 2/din (Tier A only) - foundation
- Week 3-4: 3/din (Tier A + B)
- Month 2+: 4-5/din (A + B + link-magnet outreach), never more than ~3 new directory links/day
Roz ek tracker row likho: docs/backlink-tracker.csv (Platform, URL, DR, Status, Date, Anchor, Notes).
