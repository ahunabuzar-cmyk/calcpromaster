## 1. SACH — 200k possible hai ya nahi?

**Aaj ka run-rate (latest 7-day, GSC live):** 7,220 impressions / 7 din ≈ **~31,000 / month**.
(Note: is window mein ek Reddit spike bhi shamil hai — bina spike ka base thoda kam, ~20–25k, ho sakta hai.)

**200k/month ka matlab:**

```
200,000 ÷ 30 din      = ~6,700 impressions / ROZ chahiye
Aaj                     = ~1,030 / roz
Yani chahiye            = ~6.5x jump (30 din mein)
```

**200k land karne ka exact formula:**
```
1,350 pages  ×  5 impressions/page/day  ×  30 din  =  202,500 impressions
```
Yani 200k tab aayega jab **saare 1,350 pages index hon** AUR **har page average
5 impressions/day** laye. Aaj sirf **375 pages** indexed hain aur average ~2.7
impressions/page/day hai. Yani indexed pages ~3x aur per-page rate ~1.8x karna hai.

**Isliye: 200k = "sab kuch perfect ho gaya" wala scenario.**

days" — wo ya jhoot bol raha hai ya black-hat kar raha hai.
