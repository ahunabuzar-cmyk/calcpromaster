Impressions ka **65%** position 21+ par pada hai. Wahan impression milega, click nahi — is liye impressions barhana aur clicks barhana ek hi kaam nahi hai. Jo mila wo ye bhi dikhata hai: kuch pages impress kar rahe hain jin par click **possible hi nahi** (quiz/machine-shaped queries).

_Caveat:_ ye ladder sirf un pairs par hai jo GSC ke query→page join mein aaye (12224/20954 impressions, 58%). Baaki impressions GSC ki apni truncation hain, site ka bug nahi.

_Caveat 2:_ poore 28 din mein sirf 22 clicks hain, is liye per-band CTR chhote sample par hai — 21-50 band ka 0.20% 11-20 band ke 0.10% se zyada dikhna noise hai, trend nahi. Jo cheez noise-proof hai wo hai impressions ka jhukav (65% neeche 21+).

## 7. Weekly cadence (yahi "zyada kaam" ka asli matlab hai)

**Roz (~5 min)**
1. GSC → Request Indexing, 12 URLs (`docs/REQUEST-INDEXING-TOP40.md` ke order mein). Quota khatam = ruk jao.

**Somwar (~15 min)**
1. `npm run seo:weekly` — GSC pulls + crawl boost + gates.
2. `node scripts/seo-movement-report.cjs` — dated report, pichhle hafte se compare karo.
3. `node scripts/build-query-action-plan.cjs` — top 10 work list nikalo, 10 par kaam karo.
4. `node scripts/build-growth-model.cjs` — run-rate update karo (28-day average ko run-rate na samjho).

**Decision rules**
- Run-rate (7-day) nahi barh raha + indexed% barh raha hai → problem exposure hai, crawling nahi: position/title par kaam karo.
- Indexed% ruka hua + run-rate ruka hua → crawl side: links + request-indexing + freshness.
- Koi page pos ≤ 20, ≥ 20 impressions, 0 clicks → title/meta surgery (ye sab se sasta click hai).
- Koi page 0 impressions 8+ hafte → canonical/internal-link check, warna wo page sirf index bloat hai.

## 8. Jo koi tool promise nahi kar sakta

- **30 din mein 3–4 lakh impressions/month nahi aayega.** Uske liye 9,855–13,141/din chahiye; aaj 52/din hai.
- Jo log 1–2 din mein rank karte hain: apna brand naam, bilkul uncontested long-tail, ya years purani authority. Teeno mein se koi bhi "trick" nahi hai.
- Impressions position aur indexing ke peeche aate hain, clicks uske peeche. Ye chain toot nahi sakti — is liye forecast sirf arithmetic se banta hai, wish se nahi.
- Is doc ka kaam: sahi numbers, sahi window, aur wo kaam jo asar karta hai. Baaki Google ke haath mein hai.
