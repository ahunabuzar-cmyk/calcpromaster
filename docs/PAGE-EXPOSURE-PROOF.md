## 6. Ye kya prove NAHI karta

- Ranking, clicks, CTR ya position mein koi tabdeeli. Wo sirf GSC ke fresh export se, aur kuch hafton baad, dikhega.
- Ke exposure badhane se impressions barhenge. Ye ek **mechanism** hai (content upar = crawler aur user dono tak pohanchta hai), nateeja nahi.
- Ke har page ka exposure number uski quality batata hai. Table hona aur table ka *kaam ka* hona alag cheez hai.
