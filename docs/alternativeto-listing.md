## 5. Full description (long form)
```
CalcProMaster is a free suite of 1,206 online calculators covering money, health, math, construction, education and everyday conversions — all running instantly in the browser with no signup and no paywall.

Each tool is built around the actual question people bring to it: "Can I afford this mortgage?", "How much protein do I actually need?", "How many bags of concrete for this slab?" The page answers with the number, the formula behind it, and a plain-English explanation of what the result means for your decision.

What makes it different from most calculator sites:

• Formula transparency — every calculator documents the formula it uses, its inputs, and its assumptions, so you can verify the math instead of trusting a black box.
• Worked examples — real numbers walked through step by step, not placeholder text.
• Review dates — financial and health tools carry a "last reviewed" stamp and a methodology note, so you can see when the math was last checked.
• No walls — no account, no "premium calculator" upsell, no ad covering the input field.

Highlights by category:
• Finance: mortgage, loan EMI, amortization, income tax (US/UK/CA/AU), refinance, budgeting
• Health: BMI, BMR, calorie needs, pregnancy week, body fat, ideal weight
• Construction: concrete slab & bags, gravel, excavation, paint coverage, tiles
• Education: GPA, CGPA, grade needed, percentage
• Plus unit conversion, date & time, crypto, automotive and 14 more categories.

There's nothing to install — open a tool, enter your values, get your answer. Built by a small team that keeps adding tools based on what visitors ask for.
```
