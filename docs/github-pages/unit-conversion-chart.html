Unit Conversion Chart — Exact Factors You Can Trust

Most conversion mistakes come from memorized approximations. This chart uses the exact defined factors, so a result computed here matches a result computed anywhere else. One rule drives all of it: to go from the unit with the bigger number to the unit with the smaller number, divide; to go the other way, multiply.

The multiply-or-divide rule

1 inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters. Inches are the "bigger" unit (fewer of them), centimeters the "smaller" (more of them). So cm → in means divide by 2.54, and in → cm means multiply by 2.54. Every pair below works the same way — pick the factor, then decide multiply or divide by which side you are starting from.

Temperature (not a simple multiply)

Temperature scales have different zero points, so you add or subtract before scaling:

  • Celsius → Fahrenheit: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32 (100 °C = 212 °F)
  • Fahrenheit → Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9 (32 °F = 0 °C)
  • Celsius → Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15
A quick mental check for Celsius to Fahrenheit: double the Celsius and add 30. It is accurate to within a few degrees near room temperature, but use the exact formula for cooking, medicine or science.

Worked examples

  • 10 cm to inches: 10 ÷ 2.54 = 3.937 in
  • 70 kg to pounds: 70 × 2.20462 = 154.32 lb
  • 2 US gallons to liters: 2 × 3.785411784 = 7.571 L
  • 1,600 ft² to m²: 1,600 × 0.09290304 = 148.64 m²
  • 60 mph to km/h: 60 × 1.609344 = 96.56 km/h

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert units without memorizing every factor? Multiply to go from the smaller unit to the larger-number unit and divide to go the other way.

What is the exact cm to inches factor? 1 inch is exactly 2.54 centimeters, so divide by 2.54 to get inches.

How many pounds in a kilogram? 1 kilogram is 2.20462 pounds exactly.

Is a US gallon the same as an imperial gallon? No — a US gallon is 3.785 liters and an imperial gallon is 4.546 liters.

Why do conversion results differ between websites? Rounding and chained intermediate units add error; exact factors do not.
