How to Calculate Your Exact Age in Years, Months & Days

Everyday · 4 min read · Last updated September 2026

Exact age vs. simple year subtraction

Subtracting birth year from the current year only gives a rough figure. Someone born in December 2000 is not 25 years old in January 2025 — they turn 25 in December. Exact age counts the full years, then the remaining months, then the remaining days.

The manual method, step by step

  1. Years: subtract the birth year from the current year. If your birthday has not happened yet this year, subtract 1.
  2. Months: count the months from your last birthday to the current month.
  3. Days: count the days from your last month-anniversary to today.

Worked example

Born 15 March 1998 — today is 14 September 2026

Step 1 — years: 2026 − 1998 = 28. Has 15 March happened in 2026? Yes → 28 years.

Step 2 — months: from 15 March 2026 to 15 September 2026 = 6 months.

Step 3 — days: from 15 September to 14 September = 29 days (September has 30 days) → 29 days (the 15th would complete the month).

Result: 28 years, 6 months, 29 days — one day short of 28½ years.

Do leap years matter?

For calendar age, no — a year always runs from one birthday to the next. Leap years only matter when you count total days: a February 29 birthday adds one extra day every four years, and the day counts of February (28 vs 29) change month-to-day totals.

The February 29 edge case

A person born on 29 February 2000 has had 9,131 days of life by 28 February 2025, and 9,132 days by 1 March 2025 — but which date counts as their 25th birthday? Jurisdictions differ: some treat 28 February as the legal anniversary (the day before the birth date's month-end), others 1 March. Documents that matter — pension eligibility, driving licences, contracts — follow the local legal convention, not the calendar-day count. For everyday purposes, most age calculators (including ours) step the birthday to 1 March in non-leap years, and you should match whichever convention your paperwork requires.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate exact age by hand?

Subtract the birth year from the current year for the years. If your birthday has not happened yet this year, subtract one year. Then count the remaining months from your last birthday to the current month, and the days from the last month anniversary to today.

Why is exact age different from subtracting years?

Simple year subtraction ignores whether your birthday has happened yet this year. Someone born in December 2000 is not 25 in January 2025 — they are 24 until December.

Do leap years matter for age?

For calendar age, no — a year is counted from birthday to birthday. Leap years only matter if you count exact days, where a February 29 birthday adds one extra day every four years.
