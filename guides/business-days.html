Business Days: The Math of Deadlines

Everyday · 6 min read · Last updated October 2026

Quick answer: A 30-day calendar month contains about 22 business days (30 minus 8 weekend days). Everything in project planning — lead times, SLA clocks, invoice terms — runs on this compressed calendar, and naive calendar math overestimates available work time by ~30%.

What “3 to 5 business days” actually means

This is the phrase that makes a delivery window feel vague, and it is arithmetic rather than guesswork: only Saturday and Sunday are skipped, so the same window lands on a different date depending on the weekday you start counting. Three business days from a Friday is Wednesday, not Monday, because the weekend in between does not count.

Worked example: an order placed on Friday with a "5 business days" promise arrives the following Friday — Monday counts as day 1, because the two weekend days sit outside the count. The convention is the start day is day zero and counting begins the next business day; if a promise instead counts the start day itself, every date here shifts one business day earlier.

Two patterns fall out of the rule and they answer the phrase without a calendar: 5 business days is exactly one week later and 10 is exactly two, always on the same weekday you started, while 3 business days is 3 to 5 calendar days away because it can cross a weekend. So “3–5 business days” spans roughly three to seven calendar days. Add a public holiday and every figure in the table moves one weekday later.

One named example, because the direction of the shift is what people get wrong. Five business days from Monday 23 November 2026 lands on Monday 30 November 2026 — four weekdays, then the following Monday. Put a US federal holiday inside that window, Thanksgiving on Thursday 26 November 2026, and no work happens that day, so the fifth business day becomes Tuesday 1 December 2026. A holiday can only push a date later; it can never pull one earlier. Ten business days from that same Monday is Monday 7 December 2026 — two full working weeks, exactly what the +10 column predicts, and the reason long promises are quoted in business days rather than calendar days.

Counting business days between two dates

Counted over a range, the weekday-only formula is a straight subtraction:

Business days = calendar days − weekend days − holidays

Worked example: September 2026 has 30 days containing exactly 8 weekend days (4 Saturdays + 4 Sundays) → 22 business days. Subtract a public holiday and the month delivers 21. A "one month" deadline is a 5–10% shorter clock than it feels.

Adding days to a deadline

To find a date 10 business days from a Friday: 10 workdays ≈ 2 calendar weeks, but start-day and direction matter — landing on a Saturday rolls to Monday, and a holiday inside the window pushes further. Two clocks: the shipping promise and the contractual deadline

Both are quoted in business days, and they do not start at the same moment. Carrier shipping promises are usually counted from the dispatch date, with the dispatch day itself as day zero — an order shipped on a Friday on a "3 to 5 business days" service arrives Wednesday to Friday of the following week, not Monday to Wednesday, because the weekend sits inside the window. A contractual or statutory deadline usually starts on the next business day after the notice or the event, so a letter delivered on a Friday with "respond within 10 business days" is due two full working weeks after the following Monday.

Cut-off times sit between the two: an order placed after a merchant's daily cut-off is processed the next business day, so the clock starts a day later than the order date suggests. Neither clock counts part-days — a business day is a whole day, and a promise that names a time of day is naming a cut-off, not half a day of work.

Which convention applies is written in the specific promise or contract, not in the phrase "business days" itself, so translate the wording into a start day before you count.

Holidays are local, weekends are not universal

The working week is Monday to Friday in most of Europe and the Americas, Sunday to Thursday in parts of the Gulf, and Saturday to Thursday in Israel. A count that assumes a five-day week is wrong for a large share of the world.

Public holidays are worse: they are set per country and often per state, and some move with lunar calendars. No general-purpose counter can know your jurisdiction, so treat any result near a holiday cluster as provisional.

Counting conventions change the answer by a day

Does the start date count as day zero or day one? Does a deadline that lands on a weekend move forward to Monday or back to Friday? Contracts answer these questions explicitly because the difference is a day — and sometimes a penalty.

This calculator moves weekend hits forward to the next business day, which matches the common "following business day" convention. If your contract says "preceding" or "modified following", the date shifts.

Where business-day counts actually get used

Settlement cycles, notice periods, payment terms, shipping windows and visa processing all run on business days rather than calendar days. A 30-business-day term is roughly six calendar weeks, not one month.

Half-days and bank holidays that close only part of the market are the usual source of disputes. If money movement depends on the date, confirm the calendar with the counterparty rather than relying on a generic counter.

Frequently asked questions

How many business days are in a month?

Typically 20–23. A 30-day month with 8 weekend days has 22; subtract public holidays from there.

How do I calculate business days between two dates?

Count the calendar days, subtract weekend days, subtract holidays. The Date Difference Calculator does this automatically, including weekend definitions.

What date is 10 business days from today?

Two working weeks later, so the same weekday you started counting from — under the convention used here the start day is day zero and counting begins the next business day, which is why 10 business days lands on the same weekday and 5 lands on the same weekday one week on. A public holiday inside the window shifts the date one weekday further, never earlier. Use the Date Difference Calculator for the exact date.

How many weekdays are in a year?

A 365-day year contains 261 weekdays and 104 weekend days (52 full weeks plus one extra day). A 366-day leap year contains 261 or 262 weekdays depending on which weekday it starts on. Subtract public holidays — around 11 federal holidays a year in the US — for the working-day total.

How many working days are there in a month?

The count follows the weekends: May 2026 has 31 days, 21 weekdays and 20 business days once the US Memorial Day holiday (Monday 25 May 2026) is removed. Months of 30 days with eight weekend days give 22, and a holiday inside the month takes one more.

Do all countries have the same business week?

No. Most of the world runs Monday–Friday, but several Middle Eastern countries use Sunday–Thursday or Friday–Saturday weekends. Always confirm which convention a deadline uses.
