How to Calculate a Tip — Rates, Splitting the Bill & Tipping Abroad

Everyday Money · 4 min read · Last updated September 2026

The tip formula

Tip = Bill × Tip% ÷ 100
Total = Bill + Tip · Per person = Total ÷ Guests

Mental math shortcuts

  • 10% = move the decimal one place left ($84 → $8.40)
  • 15% = 10% + half of 10% ($8.40 + $4.20 = $12.60)
  • 20% = 10% × 2 ($8.40 × 2 = $16.80)

Typical tipping ranges by service (US norms)

Outside the US, expectations differ widely: Japan often expects no tip, much of Europe rounds up or adds 5–10%, and service charges may already appear on the bill. Local norms beat any formula.

Worked example: splitting dinner

Bill $124.50, tax included, 4 friends, 18% tip on pre-tax subtotal of $115.

Tip = 115 × 0.18 = $20.70 → Total = $145.20 → Per person = $36.30

Common mistakes

  • Tip% applied after already generous rounding on the total.
  • Double-tipping when a service charge is already on the bill — read the receipt.
  • Forgetting per-person fairness when orders differ wildly — split orders first, then tip.

Splitting a bill fairly (worked example)

Dinner for five comes to $184.65 pre-tax. At 18% the tip is $33.24, making the total $217.89 — $43.58 per person if everyone splits evenly. Two fairer options when orders differ: (1) each person tips on their own subtotal before splitting anything; (2) split food evenly, then divide drinks by actual consumption. Round per-person amounts up to the nearest dollar and the table usually lands within a few dollars of exact — cover the gap yourselves rather than shorting the tip.

FAQ

Tip on pre-tax or total?

Convention is pre-tax. The difference is small but real, and servers generally expect the pre-tax basis.

Is tipping expected in Pakistan, India and the UAE?

Modest tipping is appreciated but not obligatory in Pakistan and India; UAE restaurants often add a service charge where a small extra cash tip is still customary. Follow the bill's service-charge line first.

Do I tip on discounted meals?

Etiquette guides suggest tipping on the pre-discount value — the server did the same work regardless of the coupon.
